# Bank of AMR — Security Training Lab Brief

Bank of AMR is an intentionally insecure, local-only cybersecurity training application. It is not a banking product and must never be exposed to the internet or populated with real accounts, passwords, financial data, or secrets.

The active curriculum, vulnerability verification paths, impact statements, and industry-standard remediations are maintained in `SECURITY_LAB_MATRIX.md`.

The UI has no prefilled form values or quick-access credentials. Use disposable identities and reset the local runtime data between cohorts.
